# Inside QuantumBlack

A designer field guide to how McKinsey QuantumBlack strategically
communicates, built from a BHIL STENCIL deconstruction of 32 public
artifacts published between September 2023 and September 2026.

## Why designers should study this system

QuantumBlack materials are not beautiful in the decorative sense. They are
fast. A reader can extract the argument of a 30-page survey report by
reading exhibit titles alone, and that is not an accident. It is the
product of a small set of rules applied without exception: the headline
carries the answer, every exhibit makes one point, every data visual names
its source, and named constructs arrive only after the evidence is visible.

This guide treats that discipline as a craft you can learn, the same way
pyramid-principle writing or inverted-pyramid journalism are crafts. It is
explicitly not a costume. The final chapter of every recipe in this repo
requires original names, original visual identity, and zero reproduction of
trade dress.

## The central thesis

1. **Argument first.** Every visual advances a claim, not just data.
2. **Evidence made visible.** Charts and models carry the reasoning.
3. **Methodology as credibility.** Sources, samples, and limits stay on
   the page, not in an appendix.

The key point, stated once and used everywhere: **the exhibit is the atomic
unit. One idea, one conclusion, one proof.**

## How to read this guide

Start with [the core pattern](guide/01-core-pattern.md) to see the six-step
narrative flow, then move through the visual system chapters. When you are
ready to build, the [recipes](recipes/survey-report.md) turn observations
into checklists. The [source register](registry/source-register.md) shows
exactly which public artifacts every claim rests on, with evidence tiers.

## Evidence posture

Every claim in this guide carries a tier from the STENCIL five-tier system.
Firm self-reported outcomes are STATED and quarantined. Claims resting on
fewer than three artifacts are INFERENCE by rule. The
[QA ledger](registry/qa-ledger.md) lists every flag surfaced during the
study, none silently corrected.
