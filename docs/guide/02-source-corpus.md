# Source corpus

The guide rests on 32 public artifacts across six deliverable categories,
cataloged between September 2023 and September 2026 with two flagged
pre-window foundations retained deliberately.

## Artifact mix

| Category | Count | What it contains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T1 Thought leadership | 15 | State of AI series, Technology Trends Outlook, MGI arenas reports, technical guides |
| T2 Case studies | 5 | Named client narratives and marketing case cards |
| T3 Presentations | 2 | Lawfully posted webinars and event replays |
| T4 Frameworks | 4 | Framework articles and the approach page |
| T5 Procurement | 4 | Federal and state contract records with verifiable identifiers |
| T6 Data visualization | 2 | Chart essays and week-in-charts entries |

## Headline numbers

* **32** artifacts cataloged.
* **15** long-form thought-leadership pieces anchor the corpus.
* **6** deliverable categories, all populated.
* **22 percent** of DNA claims rest on INFERENCE, under the 40 percent
  HOLD threshold.

## What the mix teaches a designer

The system is thought-leadership-led. Case evidence runs thin, several
case cards are marketing artifacts held at UNCORROBORATED, and the visual
discipline you should study lives in the survey franchise and the trend
compendium, where the exhibit machine runs at full density.

## Boundaries

Public sources only: official pages and PDFs, named public records, and
secondary corporate channels. Third-party PDF mirrors and gated copies
were excluded from structural study. The full row-level catalog with
locators, sweeps, access states, and tiers is the
[source register](../registry/source-register.md), generated from the
canonical CSV.
