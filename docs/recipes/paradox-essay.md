# Recipe: paradox essay

Turn tension into a resolution and a plan for action. Five moves, taken
from the thesis antithesis playbook pattern that carries the corpus's
biggest arguments.

## The skeleton

1. **Opening tension.** Frame the paradox in a compelling, familiar way,
   two true numbers that should not both be true.
2. **Diagnosis.** Explain the forces on both sides with evidence. This is
   where the exhibit cascade lives.
3. **Resolution framework.** Provide a structured way to navigate the
   tension. Give it an original name, and introduce it only after the
   evidence has made it feel inevitable.
4. **Governance and workforce implications.** Translate insight into
   organizational choices, who decides, who changes, what it costs.
5. **Management playbook.** Action-oriented guidance for leaders, a short
   numbered set, not a backlog.

## Density budget

Short-article class when the essay makes one decision: one to three
exhibits, text-led. Report class when the diagnosis needs a cascade.

## Craft notes

* The paradox must be measurable. "Everyone is adopting, few see impact"
  works because both halves carry numbers.
* Hedge the future, not the present. Present facts arrive low-hedge and
  sourced; projections carry ranges.
* The framework arrives late. If your named model appears before the
  evidence, you are selling, not resolving.

## Guardrail

Original framework names only. The observed constructs in
`data/constructs.csv` are competitive facts, not a menu. No firm names,
no trade dress, your byline.

## Acceptance test

Delete the framework section. If the essay still stands as diagnosis, the
framework was earned. If the essay collapses, the framework was doing
assertion work the evidence should have done.
