# The STENCIL method

STENCIL is the BHIL playbook for deconstructing a firm's public deliverable
system: Structural Template Extraction and Narrative Consulting Intelligence
Library. It runs as a fixed pipeline so two analysts produce comparable
libraries from the same corpus.

## The pipeline

| Stage | Name | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SP-1 | Scope and taxonomy | Six deliverable categories, hard scope boundary |
| SP-2 | OSINT discovery sweep | Source register with access and authorship notes |
| SP-3 | Master deliverable library | One catalog row per artifact |
| SP-4 | Narrative deconstruction | Pattern codes and section-order skeletons |
| SP-5 | Framework extraction | Construct inventory with intent and position |
| SP-6 | Visual DNA | Chart census, layout laws, density benchmarks |
| SP-7 | Lexicon and register | Register spec sheet and term migration |
| SP-8 | Codebook and prompts | Reusable recipes with guardrails |
| SP-10 | Terminal gate | SHIP, HOLD, or REWORK with rationale |

## What STENCIL extracts and what it refuses

The library holds metadata and deconstruction, not content. Structure,
sequence, framework names recorded as competitive facts, chart types, and
phrase patterns are in scope. Reproduced paragraphs are not. The quotation
budget is one illustrative quotation of at most 25 words per artifact,
cited by URL.

## The six sweeps

Discovery runs in fixed order: the target's own insight archives, operator
and PDF searches, public procurement portals, lawfully posted conference
materials, executive social link trails, and a Wayback pass for deprecated
artifacts. For this study the Wayback pass proved unnecessary because
nearly every target artifact remains live.

## Why this study scoped to QuantumBlack

McKinsey Digital and QuantumBlack publish the firm's densest visual system:
annual survey franchises, a scored trend compendium, technical Medium
posts, and case cards. That mix exercises every deliverable category in the
taxonomy, which makes it the right corpus for a designer-facing guide.

## The terminal gate for this study

Disposition SHIP: 32 artifacts against a 20 minimum, INFERENCE share of
DNA claims at 22 percent against a 40 percent HOLD trigger, IP firewall
intact, STATED quarantine verified on sampled claims, citation chain
verified on sampled rows.
